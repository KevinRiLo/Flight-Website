Program Overview

The Private Pilot Certificate (PPL) is your gateway to the world of aviation. Our comprehensive course is meticulously designed to transform aspiring flyers into skilled, responsible pilots. We focus on building a rock-solid foundation of safety, confidence, and precision. From your first takeoff to your final checkride, you'll develop core flying skills that ensure every journey is as secure as it is exhilarating.

What You Will Learn

Aircraft pre-flight inspection and safety procedures

Precision landings in various weather conditions

Emergency maneuvers and cockpit management

Mastering takeoffs, climbs, and straight-and-level flight

Cross-country navigation and route planning

Radio communications and air traffic control protocols

Course Curriculum

PHASE 01
Pre-Solo Training

Mastering the fundamentals of flight: aircraft systems, ground operations, basic maneuvers, and emergency procedures to prepare for your first solo flight.

PHASE 02
Cross-Country Navigation

Venturing beyond the local airport. Learn navigation theory, flight planning, weather analysis, and radio communications for long-distance flights.

PHASE 03
Checkride Preperation

Final refinement of flying skills and knowledge. Extensive review of FAA standards to ensure you are fully prepared to earn your Private Pilot Certificate.

Requirements

  • Be at least 17 years old to earn the certificate (16 to solo)
  • Hold a current FAA Medical Certificate (3rd Class or higher)
  • Read, speak, write, and understand the English language
  • Accumulate a minimum of 40 flight hours (national average is 60-80)
  • Pass a computer-based FAA Knowledge Test (Written Exam)
  • Successfully complete an oral and flight practical exam (Checkride)
