WHAT TO EXPECT

A discovery flight is the perfect introduction to the world of aviation. Over the course of your session, you will transition from student to pilot-in-training.

01

PRE-FLIGHT BRIEFING

Review flight basics, cockpit controls, and safety procedures. We'll walk through the pre-flight inspection together before we head to the runway.

02

HANDS-ON FLYING

Take the controls! Under expert guidance, you'll perform basic maneuvers and experience the thrill of piloting a modern aircraft yourself.

03

POST-FLIGHT ROADMAP

Debrief on your flight and receive a personalized training roadmap. This flight counts towards the logged hours for your Private Pilot Certificate.
