YOUR PATH TO THE PILOT SEAT

STEP 01

DISCOVERY FLIGHT

Your journey starts in the air. This isn't just a ride; it is your first officially logged flight lesson. You will take the controls of the aircraft, experience modern aviation firsthand, and sit down with me afterward to build a customized training roadmap.

STEP 02

FUNDAMENTALS & GROUND SCHOOL

Great pilots are built on the ground and refined in the sky. We will combine 1-on-1 ground instruction to help you easily pass the FAA Knowledge Test, while our dual flight lessons focus heavily on mastering stick-and-rudder skills and modern digital avionics.

STEP 03

FIRST SOLO & CROSS-COUNTRY

Once you demonstrate consistent, safe aircraft control, you will reach the milestone every aviator remembers: flying the aircraft entirely by yourself. From there, we transition to cross-country flying, learning to navigate the airspace and polishing your maneuvers to exact FAA standards.

STEP 04

THE CHECKRIDE

The final hurdle. You will meet with an FAA Designated Pilot Examiner (DPE) for a two-part exam: an oral interview to test your aeronautical knowledge, followed by a practical flight test. Upon a successful landing, you officially earn your Private Pilot Certificate.

STEP 05

ADVANCED RATINGS & MORE

Upon certification, your education continues with new challenges. We can transition into Instrument Ratings, Commercial licensing, or specialized training to broaden your capabilities and ensure you remain a sharp, safe pilot for life.
